NEXT Website

Website and online store for the NEXT PVP server.

Date
18/11/2026
Services
Web DesignUX/UI Design
Ask about it
01Overview

The NEXT website is the server’s front door: the first place players land before they ever join a match. The goal was to bring the energy of the NEXT brand to the web — dark, purple and competitive — while keeping everything players need one click away: game modes, live stats, rules, FAQ, a streamer area, a step-by-step tutorial and a full online store with cart and checkout.

Every page10

From the home page to checkout — drag to browse the pages and click to see them full size.

01 / 10
02Process

Designed as an extension of the game

The site reuses the same visual system as the in-game interfaces: deep blacks, a night-violet background and the NEXT purple as the only accent, so moving from browser to server feels seamless. A bold hero with the call to action and the connect command gets new players in game in seconds, while cards, filters and collapsible sections keep long content such as rules and FAQ easy to scan.

$Store strategy

A store built to sell

The online store is more than a catalogue: every detail was designed to lift conversion, reward the community and keep players coming back.

01

Creator codes

Every verified streamer gets their own code, such as NAME10. Players save 10% and the creator earns a commission — the community ends up promoting the store on its own.

02

Price-anchored packs

Five coin packs side by side. The middle one carries the “Most popular” badge and the biggest the “Best value”, with the old price struck through and bonus coins growing at every tier.

03

Discounts & gift cards

A discreet field in the cart accepts codes and gift cards. Seasonal codes — launch, double-coin weekends, Black Friday — create planned sales peaks.

04

Weekly catalogue

The in-game store refreshes every week with a clearly visible countdown. That urgency keeps players coming back so they don’t miss out.

05

Premium, compared

Free and Premium sit side by side with the perks checked off. The paid plan is highlighted in purple so the advantage is clear in a second.

06

Frictionless checkout

Cart and payment in two steps, with the total always visible and automatic delivery to the Cfx.re account and Discord. Fewer clicks, fewer abandoned carts.

03Outcome

From first visit to checkout

The result is a complete, consistent website that informs, converts and supports the community. Players discover the modes, check the leaderboard and learn the rules, while the store — with creator codes, price-anchored packs, seasonal discounts and a two-step checkout — turns visits into support for the server, all in the same language as the game they are about to play.
