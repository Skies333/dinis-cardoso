View full project
01Overview

The project for Palácio Nacional da Ajuda aimed to reinterpret historical elegance through modern design principles. The main challenge was to capture the essence of cultural heritage while ensuring a clean, contemporary aesthetic that spoke to today’s audiences. The design sought harmony between tradition and innovation, preserving the palace’s identity while giving it a refined visual update.

02Process

A refined balance of form, texture, and typography

The creative process focused on translating architectural and ornamental details into a cohesive visual language. Neutral tones, elegant typefaces, and structured compositions were chosen to echo the palace’s grandeur while maintaining visual clarity. Each design element, from printed materials to digital assets, was developed to evoke sophistication and timelessness without visual excess.

03Outcome

Timeless design for a timeless landmark

The result is a complete identity that feels both historical and modern, capturing the cultural prestige of the Palácio Nacional da Ajuda. The project celebrates craftsmanship, structure, and narrative through design, creating a visual system that not only honors the past but also redefines how heritage can be communicated in a contemporary context.
