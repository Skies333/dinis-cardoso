01Overview

The goal of the NEXT project was to design a bold and contemporary identity for a gaming community centered around GTA5 FiveM. The challenge was to create a brand and interface that reflected both the competitive energy of the platform and the welcoming, connected nature of its players. The final direction aimed to merge a clean aesthetic with dynamic visuals, ensuring an experience that felt modern, cohesive, and engaging for users.

02Process

The challenge & approach

One of the main challenges was balancing user privacy with a simple and intuitive interface. The goal was to create a clean, ad-free experience focused on meaningful interaction and accessibility. By applying a minimalist design approach, we developed a structure that allows players to easily manage their visibility and preferences, ensuring clarity, usability, and a consistent visual flow throughout the platform.

03Outcome

The result

The final outcome is a refined and focused visual system that communicates professionalism and energy through simplicity. By prioritizing clarity and consistency, the NEXT identity and interface highlight what truly matters: the community and its experience. The design successfully bridges creativity and function, embodying a clean, modern look that resonates with today’s digital audiences.
