VITAE, IADE WorkShop2022

VITAE

Reorganization and redesign of the office area.

Date
27/11/2022
Services
Interior Workspace Redesign
View full project
01Overview

This project was developed for Centro de Acolhimento VITAE, a shelter for children and young people without a home. The main challenge was to redesign the staff’s workspace, a place that lacked functionality, comfort, and organization. My goal was to transform a limited and fragmented area into a welcoming environment that encourages collaboration, focus, and well-being, reflecting the care and dedication of those who work there.

02Process

Process and Design Approach

The project began with an in-depth analysis of how the space was being used, its layout, light, and circulation. The existing configuration restricted movement and didn’t support the workflow of the staff. Based on these observations, I developed a new spatial composition that introduced better organization, modern furniture, and warmer tones. The proposal combined functionality and aesthetics, bringing together soft lighting, neutral colours, and natural textures to create balance and comfort.

03Outcome

Outcome and Impact

The final design transformed the workspace into an open, coherent, and human-centered environment. The new layout improved efficiency and atmosphere, giving the staff a more productive and motivating place to work. Beyond aesthetics, this project represents how design can genuinely contribute to social good, by shaping spaces that feel as meaningful as the work done within them.
