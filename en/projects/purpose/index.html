Purpose

Brand identity and app for home and gym workouts.

Date
18/11/2026
Services
Brand DesignUX/UI DesignApp Design
Open the app
01Overview

Purpose was born from a simple idea: a fast fitness app with practical workouts you can do at home with no equipment at all, or at the gym with the equipment that’s already there. No complications — open it, pick a workout and start. The name sums up the intention: every workout has a purpose. And the progress ring living inside the “o” of the logo reflects exactly that: it fills up with every session and reminds you there’s always one more step to reach your goal.

02Process

A logo that fills up as you train

The identity started from the name itself. The wordmark is set in Bricolage Grotesque and the “o” becomes a progress ring, filled to three quarters: a reminder that there is always one more step to go. The ring works on its own as the app icon and is animated across the product, from the opening screen to the weekly goal. The palette pairs a deep night black with an energetic lime, supported by chalk white and graphite, and Plus Jakarta Sans keeps the interface easy to read mid-workout.

03Outcome

One system, from the icon to every screen

The result is a coherent visual system that runs through the whole app: home and gym modes, a weekly plan, animated exercises, personal records and achievements. Large touch targets, high contrast and a single accent colour make it easy to use with sweaty hands and a racing heart. Purpose is live and in use — designed with intention, built to keep you coming back.
